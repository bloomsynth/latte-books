# Code of conduct

Latte Books is made by and for people who keep small businesses' books, in many countries
and languages. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere
else the project meets, is expected to make that a welcoming place.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to Rust, to accounting or to English.
- Assume good intent, and disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give and take feedback gracefully, and credit others' work.
- Respect people's privacy: never post someone's company data or personal details.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or continuing after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact a maintainer privately, through the
email on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or issue.
Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block anyone from the project,
temporarily or for good, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
